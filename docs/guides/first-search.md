# Your first search

Before this, you should have [installed and started Jobcu](install-and-start.md) and
[got your keys](getting-your-keys.md). Jobcu is open in your browser.

---

## 1. Enter your keys and choose an AI model

1. At the top right of Jobcu, click **Settings**.
2. Under **AI provider**, click the provider you got a key from.
3. **Copy your key** (from your password manager or wherever you saved it), click the **API key**
   box, paste it, and click **Save**. The box changes to "Saved (ends in ••••…)".
4. Click **Load model list**, then click the **Model** box and pick a model from the list.
   - Not sure which one? Providers usually offer a fast, low-cost model (often with names like
     "flash", "mini" or "haiku"). Those are a good start and use less of any free allowance.
5. Click **Test connection**. You should see **✓ Connection works**. If you see a message instead,
   it explains what to do.
6. Scroll down to **Job site keys**:
   - Paste your **Adzuna Application ID** → **Save**, and your **Adzuna Application Key** →
     **Save**, then click **Test Adzuna**.
   - Paste your **Reed API key** → **Save**, then click **Test Reed**.

**Never paste your keys anywhere except these boxes in Jobcu.** They're saved only on your computer.

## 2. Add your CV and cover letter

1. Click **Search** at the top.
2. Under **Your documents**, next to **CV**, click **Choose file…** and pick your CV (PDF or Word).
3. Next to **Cover letter**, click **Choose file…** and pick a cover letter (PDF, Word or a text
   file). A general one works best: say what kind of work you want and what you care about.
   A letter written for one company is fine too; Jobcu ignores the company-specific parts.
4. Optional: click **What Jobcu understood** to see how the AI read your documents. It never
   judges or changes them; it only uses this to find and score jobs.

## 3. Search

1. **Where do you want to work?** Type it in your own words, for example:
   - *Munich or within 50 km*
   - *Germany, the Netherlands or the UK*
   - leave it empty to search everywhere Jobcu can (this takes longer)
2. **Posted within:** how fresh the jobs must be. **72 hours** or **1 week** finds more jobs;
   **24 hours** shows only the very newest.
3. **Job types:** untick the types you don't want.
4. Tick **Don't include remote jobs** if you only want hybrid or on-site jobs.
5. Click **Search**.

A search takes **2–5 minutes**. You'll see each step being ticked off. If you see *"AI limit
reached, continuing more slowly"*, that's normal on a free AI allowance: Jobcu waits and carries
on by itself.

If Jobcu asks whether to **score more jobs** (it stops at a limit to protect your AI allowance),
choose **Score … more** for a complete list, or **Show results now**.

## 4. Understand the results

Each card shows:

- **The score** (0–100): how well the job fits you. Low scores aren't hidden; they're just lower
  in the list. Hover over the score to see its parts.
- **New**: you haven't seen this job in an earlier search.
- Company, place, job type, and when it was posted (e.g. "posted 3 hours ago").
- **Reasons**: the most important strengths and gaps, e.g. "Asks for 5+ years".
- **✓ In Germany (verified: …)**: the job's country, as stated by the job site.
- **? Scored from a short summary of the ad**: the full ad couldn't be read, so the score is less
  certain.
- **Open job**: opens the ad. **Also on** shows other sites with the same job.
- **Possible duplicate**: probably the same job posted by a staffing agency.

Buttons on each card:

- **Save**: keep it in your **Saved** list (top of the results).
- **Applied**: mark jobs you applied to; they appear in your **Applied** list.
- **Not interested**: hides the job now and in every later search. Tick **Show hidden** to undo.

**Search details** (next to the Search button) shows each job site's results, how many jobs were
left out and why, the search words Jobcu used in each language, and how much AI the search used.

Your last results stay available after you close Jobcu.
